Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-02

VOID Launcher ("we", "us", or "our") provides a launcher app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the app and your rights under applicable privacy laws.

1. Data We Process

  • Installed app metadata: app names, package names, and launch intents needed to display your app list and launcher features.
  • Usage statistics (if granted): app usage time and screen-time related values to support timers and productivity insights.
  • Local preferences: launcher layout, favorites, theme, settings, and wallpaper selections stored on your device.
  • Diagnostics and analytics: crash reports and aggregated analytics (for example, Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics), when enabled in your build.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not read your private messages, photos, or contacts for launcher operation.
  • We do not collect precise location for launcher functionality.

3. Why We Process Data

  • To provide core launcher features, app indexing, and app launch actions.
  • To enable optional usage-based features (limits, stats, reminders).
  • To improve reliability, fix crashes, and prevent abuse.

4. Android Permissions and Special Access

VOID Launcher requests only what is needed for launcher and screen-time features. Below is what we use, whether it is required, and why.

Permission / access Required? Why we use it
Installed apps / package visibility
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES		 Yes (launcher core) To show your full app list on the home screen, search, and favorites, and to open apps when you tap them. We do not sell this list.
Default Home launcher role Optional (recommended) So VOID can act as your Android home screen. You choose this in system settings; you can switch back to another launcher anytime.
Usage access
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS		 Optional To read per-app screen time for home stats, timers, and daily limits. Granted only if you turn on Usage access in Android settings.
Notifications
POST_NOTIFICATIONS		 Optional To show alerts when app limits or reminders apply. You can deny or revoke this permission.
Photos / storage
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Optional Only if you pick a custom wallpaper from your gallery. We do not scan your entire library in the background.
Biometric unlock
USE_BIOMETRIC (device)		 Optional To protect access to hidden-apps vault settings when you choose biometric lock instead of a PIN.
Foreground service
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC		 When limits are active To monitor app time limits reliably while you use your phone, with a visible ongoing notification where required by Android.
Boot completed
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED		 When limits are active To restore limit monitoring after you restart your device.
Background processes
KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES		 When limits are active To pause or block an app when its daily limit is reached, as part of screen-time controls you configured.

We do not request location, contacts, SMS, call logs, or microphone access for launcher operation. Phone/dialer intents are used only when you tap the Phone shortcut to open your device dialer.

5. Legal Basis and Consent

We process data required for core app functionality based on legitimate interest and contract-like necessity to provide the service. Optional permissions and special access (usage access, notifications, photos, biometrics) are used only after you grant them and can be revoked in Android settings at any time.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Most launcher data is stored locally on your device. Crash and analytics events, if enabled, may be processed by trusted service providers (such as Google Firebase). Data is retained only as long as needed for product, legal, and security requirements.

7. Data Sharing

We share data only with service providers required to operate and improve the app (for example hosting, analytics, crash reporting), and only to the extent necessary.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; therefore absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under the age required by local law. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us to request removal.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data processing. You may also revoke permissions from Android settings at any time.

11. International Transfers

Service providers may process data in countries outside your own. Where required, we rely on contractual or legal safeguards for cross-border transfers.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, additional in-app notice.
